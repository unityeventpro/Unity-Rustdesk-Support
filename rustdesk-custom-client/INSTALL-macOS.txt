Permanent-password access uses the owner-approved support password. One-time
password authentication is disabled by default. Obtain the permanent password
directly from your authorized technician; it is not printed here. Existing
locally saved passwords and network/security settings take priority.

macOS requires Screen Recording and Accessibility/Control approval in System
Settings > Privacy & Security before a technician can view/control your screen.
A signed package does not grant or bypass those permissions. Reopen the app if
macOS requests it after permissions change. Keep the Mac awake and the app or
its background service running for remote access.

The launcher uses the enlarged Unity logo with Unity AV Support beneath it.
RustDesk app/bundle/service identity is retained for compatibility. Inventory
and heartbeats register in the Unity dashboard without a client dashboard login.

SHA256SUMS and the Mac build manifest identify the final release. A final release
is only marked notarized after Apple's acceptance, stapling and Gatekeeper checks.
